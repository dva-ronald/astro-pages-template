---
title: Tokens before utilities
description: Why the Astra template asks AI and humans to restyle through CSS variables first.
pubDate: 2026-11-01
---

When a new site starts from this template, the fastest win is almost never a rewrite of every block.

Change the tokens: display and body fonts, the ink/fog/accent palette, section spacing, and radii. Parent themes (`theme-light`, `theme-dark`, `theme-brand`) cascade into sections automatically.

Only after the atmosphere feels right should you introduce a new block variant — and only when an existing variant cannot carry the layout.

That order keeps Pages CMS schemas stable while still leaving room for expressive design.
